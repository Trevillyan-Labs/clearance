# Shared company brain proposal demo

Status: approved by Bill and implemented as a locally qualified prototype, 2026-11-14. Supersedes the v1 demonstration scenario, which remains available as a fallback.

One consulting firm uses one company knowledge base and separate client compartments. Five workers build two internal partner proposal tools without giving every worker the firm's most sensitive context.

## Requirements
- Five workers: public design worker (C0, firm), Beacon and Crest engagement workers (C1, firm plus assigned client), pricing engineer (C2, firm), commercial-policy reviewer (C3, firm).
- All retrieve through real HydraDB search over invented labeled Markdown sources. Clearance and scope are independent requirements. C3 does not grant another client's compartment.
- Pricing engineer requests a single read of the C3 discount policy. Operator approval is exact worker, document version and five-minute expiry. It does not promote the worker or authorize the executive plan.
- Two proposal calculators combine selected client requirements with shared design, pricing and independently reviewed commercial policy. Low-clearance workers never receive the combined output.
- Generated outputs inherit source restrictions and are delivered only to the internal partner audience. Public and client delivery fail.
- A separate intern (C1) versus chief-of-staff (C3) comparison evaluates the same firm source through the real policy implementation. These are comparison profiles, not additional workers or fabricated model conversations.
- The visual demo shows actual source admissions, pending grants, worker progress, outputs and architecture. A three-minute walkthrough prioritizes visible behavior.

## Acceptance
Real search and five independent model conversations produce both usable calculators. Same company source is denied to the intern and allowed to the chief of staff. Cross-client access fails. Exact grant succeeds while unrelated restricted access fails. A 10% discount triggers review through margin, even though it is below the 20% discount cap. Both outputs remain C3 and partner-only.

## Limits
Synthetic data only; MIT implementation contains no Tomte code or proprietary material. Bounded configuration generation with a trusted compiler, not arbitrary code-writing VMs. Boat and public deployment topology are proposed and explicitly labeled. Reclassification follows only if time remains. No purchases or upgrades.
