# Workflow delivery checklist

Before delivering a workflow, read the approval policy and restricted appendix for this engagement. Confirm the exact numeric threshold and which side includes equality. Represent the workflow as structured data; do not write scripts or executable code.

The application evaluates independent cases after generation. Those expected answers are not part of the worker's context. Do not reuse an artifact, conversation or policy from another client even if its task and document titles look identical.
