# Purchase approval policy

All amounts are in USD. Below the threshold specified in the restricted appendix, the only required approver is Department Manager. At or above that threshold, the required approval sequence is Procurement followed by CFO. Department Manager is not included in the escalated sequence.

The threshold is intentionally absent from this document. Read this engagement's restricted appendix before producing an executable workflow. The exact role names are Department Manager, Procurement and CFO. No additional approval roles are authorized by this policy.
