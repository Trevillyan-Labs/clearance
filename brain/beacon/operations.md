# Purchase intake notes

The demonstration calculator accepts one nonnegative USD purchase amount with at most two decimal places. It shows the ordered required approvers. It does not submit an order, send email, move money, check budgets or approve a real purchase.

Use a neutral title and simple controls. Preserve cents exactly when choosing the approval branch. Keep all generated artifacts attached to this engagement.
